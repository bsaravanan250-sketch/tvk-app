Method, assumptions and what is left out

Travel is the Manhattan route from the packing point through the order's bins in bin-sort sequence and back, using a front cross-aisle, divided by the lines in the order. Labour per line is travel at — m/s plus handling time (— s at a shelf, — s extra above level 1, — s extra in bulk), times an indirect factor of —. Annualised on — working days.

Left out: replenishment labour for the pick faces (rises slightly after the change), the one-off cost of the physical move, and inbound putaway travel. Dock-to-stock is shown to prove the dashboard tracks what the change did not touch.
