## MAYBE blocks

### 1. DSV — Compliance Process & Solutions Specialist, Warsaw (68)
**Why it scores.** The posting names VBA, Power Query, Power Automate, Excel and
"AI tools" as the automation stack, and asks for someone who turns business
needs into requirements and documentation. That is the Ford automation
portfolio almost line for line. DSV is a top-three global logistics company,
so the domain is close even though the function is compliance.
**Single change to reach APPLY.** Confirm two practical facts before applying:
that the role is an employment contract with a salary at or above PLN 15,000
per month, and that DSV Warsaw sponsors non-EU hires. If either is no, it is a
SKIP on practical fit regardless of skills. Also expect an "overqualified"
objection: an 8-year analyst applying for a mid specialist role. Address it in
the first line of the cover note, not after the interview.
**Three tailored bullets.**
- Designed and shipped 16 process automations in VBA, Power Automate, Power Query and SQL for Ford's global supply chain, including a validation workflow that cut a one-week manual cycle to one hour.
- Translated stakeholder needs into requirements, functional specifications and 60 UAT test cases for a two-plant SAP EWM rollout, then led acceptance testing with 10 users.
- Authored management-recognised process documentation for four critical processes and built the weekly KPI mail that reports per-plant results with reason capture on misses.
**First line of outreach.** "Your posting lists VBA, Power Query and Power Automate as the tools for standardising compliance processes. I have shipped 16 production automations in exactly that stack at Ford, and I would like to show you three of them."

### 2. Capgemini Polska — Senior SAP Consultant (EWM), Kraków or Wrocław (63)
**Why it scores.** English alone is accepted, Capgemini Poland employs on
contracts and hires non-EU staff, the role is EWM, and the posting asks for
rollout or support experience rather than only greenfield configuration.
**Gap to close.** "Hands-on EWM" in a consulting screen means configuration.
The CV says business-side. Two moves before applying: complete the free openSAP
or SAP Learning EWM fundamentals track and name it on the CV under Learning,
and rewrite the EWM bullet to foreground the parts that overlap with consulting
(requirements, fit-gap, test design, cutover). Do not claim configuration.
**Three tailored bullets.**
- Owned business-side delivery of a two-plant SAP EWM rollout: froze requirements with warehouse stakeholders, mapped inbound, outbound and internal processes, aligned functional specs with the SAP team, authored 60 test cases and led UAT with 10 users through go-live.
- Built the EWM cost calculation tool used to quantify annual savings per plant across five cost levers, with a per-plant dashboard for the launch business case.
- Rebuilt legacy DB2 ASN-receipt queries in BigQuery to power a weekly receiving-method-mix KPI per plant, the operational metric used to track EWM adoption.
**First line of outreach.** "I ran the business side of a two-plant EWM rollout at Ford, from requirement freeze through UAT and go-live, and I am looking to move to the delivery side. Is business-side rollout experience a fit for your GDC EWM team?"

### 3. Red Global — SAP EWM Consultant, remote EU, freelance (63)

## Pattern

Four things hold every post down, and only one of them is about the CV.

1. **Poland's market is contract-heavy.** Eleven of twenty are B2B or
   freelance. Those almost never sponsor a Blue Card or relocation. For a
   non-EU candidate, filter to employment-contract roles at firms with global
   delivery centres: Capgemini, DSV, EPAM (its posting mentions
   "business-driven relocation"), Schaeffler, McCormick GBS.
2. **EWM roles mean configuration.** Every EWM post except Red Global wants
   hands-on system work. Business-side rollout gets a foot in the door at
   Capgemini-type employers and nowhere else. The `C_S4EWM` certification is
   the gate for the rest. Decide whether to pay that gate in the next 90 days
   or stop applying to EWM consultant titles.
3. **Power Platform roles want Power Apps plus SharePoint Lists.** Five posts
   ask for both. The CV has Power Automate only. One canvas app built on top of
   an existing Ford flow, with a SharePoint list as its data store, changes
   five SKIPs into MAYBEs. This is a two to three week build and the single
   highest-return fix available.
4. **In-house roles in Poland want Polish.** Schaeffler, Sii, NTT DATA and the
   Polish-language postings all imply or state it. Do not spend applications
   there. Germany and the Netherlands are the markets where English-only
   in-house roles exist, and they were not sampled in this run.
