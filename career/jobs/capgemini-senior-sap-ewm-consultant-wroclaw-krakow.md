 Your role
 As a Senior SAP EWM Consultant, you design and deliver future-ready warehouse and logistics solutions for international clients. Acting as a trusted advisor, you translate business needs into scalable SAP solutions, take ownership of key functional areas, and contribute to process optimization and integration decisions.
 You will also have the opportunity to develop your expertise in AI and explore its potential within SAP and logistics, building skills in an increasingly important area of SAP transformation.
 Your project
 Join the GDC (Global Delivery Center) and become a part of the PBS (Package Based Services) department, a team of over 300 SAP specialists. We combine deep technological expertise with strategic business consulting, delivering innovative solutions to global enterprises, including Fortune 500 companies. Working across diverse industries, we support our clients in achieving their business transformation goals.
 Your tasks
 take an active role in SAP EWM implementation, transformation and support projects for international clients;
 analyze complex warehouse and logistics processes and translate business requirements into scalable SAP EWM solutions;
 take ownership of selected functional and solution areas, from requirements analysis and design through implementation and stabilization;
 advise clients on process optimization, system enhancements and SAP best practices;
 contribute to integration and solution design decisions across SAP EWM and related modules;
 collaborate closely with clients, architects and international project teams as a trusted SAP logistics expert;
 build knowledge around AI and its potential use cases within SAP and logistics processes, with opportunities to develop further in this area.
 Your profile
 several years of hands-on experience in SAP EWM, gained in implementation, rollout or support projects;
 strong understanding of end-to-end warehouse and logistics processes;
 experience in solution design and translating business requirements into SAP solutions;
 good knowledge of integration with other SAP modules such as MM, SD, PP and TM;
 ability to work independently, take ownership of functional areas and advise clients on best-fit solutions;
 strong communication skills and confidence in working with international clients and cross-functional teams;
 proficiency in English or German (German is a strong advantage), combined with an interest in AI and emerging technologies within the SAP ecosystem.
