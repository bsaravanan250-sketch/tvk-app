How this works

Where does my complaint go?

It's logged with an ID, reviewed at the ward office, and forwarded to the government department that owns the issue — GCC, Metrowater or TANGEDCO. You see every step in the tracker.

How long does resolution take?

Average is 2.3 days. If nothing moves in 7 days, use Escalate in the tracker — it goes to the Zonal Officer with your evidence attached.

Is my phone number shared?

Only with the ward office and the assigned field officer, for site visits and closure confirmation.

Can I complain without the app?

Yes — call 1913 (GCC) or 1100 (CM Helpline) and quote the same issue. The app just makes tracking easier.
